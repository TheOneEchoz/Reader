Files in this folder are used by the experimental "Use AI to keep whole objects together" option of Find variants.
They are downloaded only when that option is used, and then kept on the device.

mobilesam_encoder.onnx, mobilesam_decoder.onnx
  MobileSAM (https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM), Apache License 2.0.
  Converted to ONNX and partly quantized to 8 bits for Tankobon.
  MobileSAM builds on Segment Anything by Meta AI, Apache License 2.0.

ort.wasm.min.js, ort-wasm-simd-threaded.mjs, ort-wasm-simd-threaded.wasm
  ONNX Runtime Web 1.30.0 (https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), MIT License, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.
